This program simulates a rideshare system along a 31-mile track. There are stations every mile, totaling 32 stations. There are also 50 passengers randomly spread among these stations. The simulation runs twice, once with 20 cars randomly spread and once with 40 cars. Each car can hold 3 passengers. Both the passengers and cars have a destination. The cars will move through the stations to their destinations, picking up and dropping off passengers along the way, and eventually dropping all passengers off when the cars reach their destination. 

After running the simulation 10 times, the average percent of passengers who completed their trips when there were 20 cars was 51.6%. The average for 40 cars was 72.0%.

For me, the project was quite challenging but enjoyable. I struggled with initially figuring out how I wanted to do the project, then at the end with the bug fixing needed to make it work properly. I endured, however, and I am proud of my completed work.
